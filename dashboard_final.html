◈ Ad Intelligence — Definitions
FILTER THRESHOLDS
Min impressions for an ad to be evaluated
ROAS ≥ this value = Winner (green)
AD ↔ ORDER MATCHING ALGORITHM
Step 1 — Build UTM Map from Orders:
For each Shopify order, extract utm_content from customAttributes. Group orders by utm_content to build a lookup of {utm_content → orders, revenue, products}.

Step 2 — Match Meta Ad Name → utm_content:
For each Meta ad, try to match its Ad Name against the utm_content map using: ① Exact match, ② URL-decoded match, ③ Partial/substring match.

Step 3 — Compute ROAS:
ROAS = Matched Revenue ÷ Ad Spend
Revenue comes from the sum of order totals (net of refunds/discounts) where utm_content matched the ad name.
ROAS COLOR CLASSIFICATION
Winner — ROAS ≥ 3.0x (profitable, scale up)
Breakeven — ROAS ≥ 1.0x but below target (covers cost, needs optimization)
Losing — ROAS < 1.0x (spending more than earning, pause or rework)
No Data — No matched orders yet (new ad or UTM mismatch)
PRODUCT DETECTION
Primary: From matched orders → most purchased product in those orders.
Fallback: If no order match, scan the Ad Name for known product names or slugs from the catalog (~250 products).
